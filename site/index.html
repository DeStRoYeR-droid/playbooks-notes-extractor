Your Play Books highlights, as clean Markdown

Google Play Books saves your notes as a cluttered Google Doc. Drop its Markdown export here and get back only what you marked: the highlighted passages and your comments, grouped by chapter or by highlight color.

  1. Drop the export. The .md file Google Docs downloads for your book's notes.
  2. Pick a layout. By chapter, by color, or color then chapter; bullets or quotes.
  3. Download. A tidy Book title - Notes.md, ready for Obsidian or any notes app.

or

The .md file from Google Docs, up to 10 MB. It is read in your browser and never uploaded.

Where do I get this file?
  1. In Play Books, open the book's notes settings and turn on saving notes to Google Drive.
  2. In Google Drive, open the doc named “Notes from book title”.
  3. In Google Docs, choose File, then Download, then Markdown (.md).

    How it works
    
      
    Play Books keeps every note in a Google Doc in your Drive. This page reads that doc's Markdown download, keeps only what you wrote and marked, and tidies the rough edges of the export.
    

      
    
        
    
          
    Get the export

    What gets tidied
    
        
    When a highlight runs across a column or page break, Play Books stores it as two notes. They are joined back together when they share a chapter, color and date and the first stops mid-sentence. A passage highlighted twice is kept once, and the export's backslash escapes are removed.
    
        
    
          
          
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Your notes stay on your device
    
        
    The file is read by your browser and converted there. Nothing is uploaded, stored or tracked. The only thing it remembers is your light or dark theme choice.
